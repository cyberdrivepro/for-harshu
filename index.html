Dear Harshu,

Main jaanta hoon ki shayad ab meri baaton ya mere hone ki koi value nahi bachi tumhare liye. Shayad tum mujhe ab kuch bhi nahi manti, aur tumhare dil mein mere liye sab khatam ho chuka hai. Lekin main yeh sab tumhe kisi tarah convince karne ya manane ke pressure se nahi keh raha. Main bas isliye yeh likh raha hoon kyunki jo maine gusse mein kaha, woh meri sabse badi galti thi — aur mujhe uska sach mein dil se afsos hai.

Jab humare beech sab bigad raha tha, main apne darr aur apne dard ko sambhal nahi paaya. Maine keh diya, “Jao, mujhe bhi nahi rehna.” Sach yeh hai ki main tumhe khona nahi chahta tha. Lekin us darr ka matlab yeh nahi tha ki main tumse us tarah rude behave karun. Jo dard mera tha, uska dukh maine tumhe diya.

Aaj sochta hoon toh lagta hai, mujhe apni baat jeetne ki jagah us pal ko sambhalna chahiye tha. Thoda rukna chahiye tha. Tumhe sunna chahiye tha. Jo baat narmi se kahi jaa sakti thi, maine use gusse mein keh kar sab tod diya.

Tumhari yaad aati hai. Tumhare saath ki chhoti si baatein bhi mere liye chhoti nahi thi. Main un yaadon ko pyaar se rakhna chahta hoon. Un achchhe palon ke liye thank you. Unka apna ek soft corner rahega.

Jab doori badhi aur jawab aane band ho gaye, main darr ke maare baar-baar koshish karta raha. Ab mujhe samajh aaya ki meri urgency tumhari zimmedari nahi ban sakti. Agar tum mujhe pehle jaisa nahi maanti, toh woh tumhara haq hai. Main tumhari marzi ko respect karta hoon.

Main sirf apni galti kehna nahi, usse seekhna bhi chahta hoon. Apne shabdon mein zyada tehzeeb, gusse ke beech ek pause, aur kisi ki baat sunne ki patience. Main bas ek better insaan banne ki koshish kar raha hoon.

Agar kabhi tum khud baat karna chaho, main bina kisi argument ke sununga. Aur aaj agar tum bas yeh letter padhkar band karna chaho, woh bhi theek hai. Tumhari khushi aur tumhara sukoon mere liye sabse zaroori hai.

I'm sorry, Harshu. Jo kehna pehle chahiye tha, woh aaj dil se keh raha hoon. Tumhe turant reply karne, mujhe maaf karne, ya kuch decide karne ki koi majboori nahi hai.
