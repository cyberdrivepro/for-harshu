Pata hai, jab se tune baat karni band ki hai na, din nikal toh jata hai par kisi cheez me mann nahi lagta. Har baar lagta hai shayad abhi phone me tera notification aa jaye. Maine gusse me bol diya 'Jao mujhe nahi rehna', par sach ye hai ki main tere bina reh hi nahi sakta. Main darr gaya tha ki kahi tu mujhse door na chali jaye, aur us darr ke chakkar me maine khud hi tujhe itna hurt kar diya. Fir jab tu chup hui toh main baar-baar text karke tujhe aur irritate karta raha.

Mujhe pata hai mera gussa kitna kharab hai, aur maine jo bhi kaha usne tujhe bohot zyada dukh diya. Par Harshu, mera irada tujhe hurt karne ka kabhi nahi tha. Main bas darr gaya tha ki main tujhe kho na doon, aur usi bewakoofi me maine tujhe itna door kar diya.

Main perfect nahi hoon Harshu, galti hui mujhse, bohot badi galti hui. Par main tujhe khona nahi chahta. Agar tujhe time chahiye, tu le le. Daantna hai, daant le. Par please humari is dosti aur rishte ko aise khatam mat hone de.

Tu mere liye kitni special hai na Harshu, ye mujhe tab aur zyada samajh aaya jab tune baat karna band kar diya. Har choti cheez mujhe teri yaad dilati hai — koi gana sunta hoon toh tera khayal aata hai, koi funny cheez dekhta hoon toh sabse pehle tujhe batane ka mann karta hai.
