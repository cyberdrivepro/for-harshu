FOR HARSHU — A SOFTER BEGINNING

OPEN THE WEBSITE
Unzip the whole folder and open index.html. Keep assets/, photos/, and soundtrack.mp3 beside it. Everything needed is included. No installation or build is required.

PERSONALIZE
Edit CONFIG in index.html for herName, yourName, six photo paths/captions, and secretMessage. Search SECRET MESSAGE — EDIT BELOW. The secret message is inside backticks; avoid literal backticks or ${ sequences when editing it. The envelope reveals a message visually; it is not password protection or encryption.

PHOTOS
Add photos/photo1.jpg through photos/photo6.jpg, or change CONFIG.photos. Until real photos are added, the gallery shows honest pastel memory spaces. No couple photos have been invented. Tap a memory for the preview; Previous/Next, keyboard arrows, and horizontal swipes work.

SCENES AND COLORS
Choose Rose Morning, Blue Clouds, or Quiet Garden on the opening section. The settings menu has Rose Pink, Sky Blue, Soft Lavender, and Warm Peach palettes. New original scenes include a rose dawn and a peaceful garden beside water. They are decorative illustrations, not places claimed as personal memories. See assets/art-notes.txt for the art prompts.

MUSIC AND READING
Two local instrumental melodies are included. Sound starts OFF every visit. Choose a melody and volume in settings. The page pauses audio when hidden. Lights and floating hearts switch separately. Pause animations respects device reduced-motion settings. Quiet reading hides decorations, and Larger text increases the letter and diary text.
Open my little diary reads the same apology letter over five pages. Previous/Next and keyboard arrows work; the diary never turns pages automatically.

THE FINAL RESTART INVITATION AND REPLY
The last section asks about a gentle restart, only if both people want it. The reader can choose a conversation, time/space, or no restart, or write only their own words. No option is selected automatically. All options support the same reply actions.
The preview shows exactly the reply that Copy, Share, or Download uses. Copy uses the clipboard when available, with a manual-copy fallback. Share opens the device's own share menu when supported; the reader chooses the app and recipient. Download creates my-reply.txt.
Replies are NOT automatically sent, uploaded, emailed, or collected in an inbox. To receive a reply, the reader must share it with you themselves. The page does not confirm delivery. No contact destination was supplied, so none has been guessed.
Draft text stays on the page by default. The reader can explicitly choose to remember it on that device. Clear draft also attempts to remove the saved device copy. On a shared device, leave Remember unchecked. Scene/color/music/decor preferences are saved separately. Music never autoplays.

LIGHTWEIGHT DELIVERY
New scenes are optimized WebP images. The garden images and photos load lazily; the opening image has high priority. Audio uses preload=none. Fonts and all assets are local. Decorations have a small fixed count, animations mainly use transforms/opacity, and hidden-page animation pauses. There are no trackers, remote fonts, third-party embeds, autoplay, or polling loops.
